SHINRAI CAT CAFÉ SIGN — V11 READABILITY / REPLICA FIX

WHY V10 HAD NO VISIBLE WRITING IN GAME
The text geometry existed, but it was physically much too small.

The supplied master reference was measured directly:
- Japanese 猫カフェ is roughly 0.33 m wide x 0.10 m high on the 0.62 m sign.
- V10 used only about 0.23 m x 0.05 m.
- V10 Japanese tube diameter was only about 2.4 mm, which became sub-pixel
  at the gameplay distance shown in the screenshot.

V11 FIX
- 猫カフェ: about 0.332 m wide x 0.097 m high
- Japanese visible tube diameter: about 10.6 mm
- underline: about 0.302 m wide
- CAT CAFE: about 0.232 m wide x 0.031 m high
- cat body outline: about 0.34 m wide x 0.24 m high
- whisker-to-whisker width: about 0.43 m

These measurements are based on the supplied master front-view panel rather
than eyeballing the proportions.

INSTALL
1. Close Godot.
2. Extract this ZIP into your Shinrai project root.
3. Choose Replace files in destination.
4. Reopen Godot and let the GLB reimport.
5. Run the game.

ONLY THE SIGN ASSET IS REPLACED.
Your current placement script, main.tscn, town_main.gd and Yakitori work are
not changed.
