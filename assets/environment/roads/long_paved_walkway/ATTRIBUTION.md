## Modifications made for Project Shinrai

The supplied Sketchfab GLB was modified for use in the Godot project:

- Reoriented to the project's Godot convention: **X = width, Y = up, Z = length**.
- Recentered on X/Z and grounded so the lowest point is at Y = 0.
- Reduced render geometry from **361,165 triangles** to **54,170 triangles** while preserving texture coordinates and normals.
- Downscaled all embedded textures from **4096 × 4096** to **2048 × 2048**.
- Re-encoded base-color and normal textures as JPEG and retained metallic/roughness/occlusion data losslessly as PNG.
- Retained the two original PBR material sets.

The original uploaded GLB includes its own asset metadata identifying SPLEEN VISION as the author, the Sketchfab source URL above, and the CC BY 4.0 license.
