# Somei-yoshino (Prunus × yedoensis) — modelling reference

The cherry of Tokyo's parks (Ueno, Chidorigafuchi, Shinjuku Gyoen). Every planted tree is a graft clone, so they all look alike and bloom together.

## Whole tree
- **Size in a park, mature (30–60 years):** 8–12 m tall, often as wide as tall or wider. A 30–40 ft (9–12 m) height with a 20–50 ft (6–15 m) spread is typical. Old Tokyo trees spread wider than they are tall.
- **Form:** a short trunk that splits low (1.5–2.5 m) into 3–5 big leaders. They rise at about 40–60° and then arch out nearly level. The crown is a broad dome or umbrella, flat-ish on top. Lower limbs reach far out horizontally, and their tips droop a little under the bloom.
- **Branching:** from leader to limb to branch to twig is 4–5 orders. It is sympodial and zig-zag: each year's twig bends slightly at the bud. The outer crown is a dense mesh of thin twigs, 3–8 mm thick. Branches thin steadily; there are never blunt ends.
- **Seen from below:** a dark lattice of limbs and twigs, every twig loaded with flowers. The structure shows through the bloom everywhere, most clearly toward the trunk.

## Flowers (the key to "growing from the branches")
- **Bloom comes before the leaves.** At full bloom there are no leaves, only a few tiny green-red leaf buds at most.
- **Where flowers grow:** from lateral buds along the previous year's twigs and from short spurs on older wood. Each bud opens into a cluster (a congested corymb/raceme) of **4–6 flowers, sometimes 3**.
- **Pedicels (flower stalks):** **2–2.5 cm**, hairy, so the flowers hang slightly out and down from the twig in a loose ball. A cluster is about **8–12 cm** across.
- **Flower:** **3–3.5 cm** across (up to 4 cm). Five rounded petals, each with a small **notch at the tip**. It is a flat, open saucer.
- **Colour:** opens **pale pink**, fades to **nearly white** at full bloom with a faint pink blush. The centre and stamens deepen to a **pink-red eye** as the flower ages. The calyx tube is about 7–8 mm and reddish-brown and hairy, so from below the cluster bases look darker reddish.
- **Density:** clusters every few cm along the outer twigs, so the outer crown reads as solid soft pink-white. Toward the trunk on older limbs, flowers sit only on short spurs, so the structure shows.

## Bark
- **Colour:** reddish/purplish-brown to dark grey-brown, **glossy**.
- **Lenticels:** prominent **horizontal lenticel bands** (corky lines) across the trunk and limbs.
- **Texture:** smooth to finely rough. On old trunks it splits in horizontal strips and has lichen patches.
- **Young twigs:** slender, reddish-brown and shiny.

## Ground under the tree
- Petals fall as **single petals** (each about 1.5 cm, notched), not whole flowers. They build a pale pink-white drift densest at the drip line and under the crown, and they collect in hollows and along path edges.

## What this means for the game model
1. Flowers come in clusters of 4–6, each flower about 3–3.5 cm, on visible 2–2.5 cm stalks fanning out from a single bud point on a twig. Never single cards floating free of a twig.
2. Clusters sit every few cm along all outer twigs, thinner toward the trunk.
3. The trunk forks low into 3–5 leaders that arch out to a near-horizontal spread, and the crown is wider than it is tall.
4. Twigs go 4–5 orders deep and zig-zag. Every branch tapers into twigs.
5. The bark is glossy red-brown to grey-brown with horizontal lenticel bands.
6. No leaves at full bloom.
7. Colour is near-white with a pale pink blush, slightly deeper pink at the centres and calyces.
